Important Disclaimer — Please Read Before Continuing

This Athletics Aid Estimator is intended to provide a general estimate only of how athletic and academic aid may apply toward your cost of attendance at Boise State University for the 2026–2027 academic year.

This tool does not constitute an official financial aid offer. All figures shown are based on average costs and may not reflect your individual cost of attendance. Actual costs may vary based on your specific program, course selections, section enrollment, and individual circumstances.

Academic scholarship awards are subject to eligibility requirements, application deadlines, and GPA thresholds. Meeting minimum GPA requirements does not guarantee an award. Contact the BSU Office of Financial Aid for official scholarship information.

Athletic aid is governed by NCAA regulations and Boise State University institutional policy. Only the official Statement of Athletics Financial Aid signed by an authorized athletics administrator constitutes an official offer of athletic aid.

International student-athletes may be subject to U.S. federal income tax on room, board, and cost of attendance portions of athletic aid received. Please consult a tax professional for guidance.

For official cost of attendance figures and financial aid information, please visit boisestate.edu/financialaid or contact the Athletics Financial Aid Office directly.

By continuing, you acknowledge that this tool provides estimates only and does not represent an official offer of financial aid.
